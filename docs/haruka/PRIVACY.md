# Haruka privacy policy

*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

Haruka is a personal, open-source manga reader for Android, based on [Mihon](https://github.com/mihonapp/mihon). It has no server and no accounts of its own.

## What Haruka collects

Nothing. Haruka has no analytics, advertising or telemetry in its published builds, and its developer receives no data about you or your device.

## Data on your device

Your library, reading progress, history, downloads, backups and settings are stored on your device. They leave it only when you choose to: by creating a backup, by using a tracker you log in to, or by turning on sync.

## Google Drive sync (optional)

If you turn on sync and sign in with Google, Haruka asks for one permission: `drive.appdata`. This lets Haruka store its own sync files in a hidden application folder in **your** Google Drive. It does not let Haruka see, read or change any of your other Drive files.

- The sync files hold your library, categories, reading progress, history, tracker links and Haruka settings, so your devices can share them.
- They are stored only in your Google Drive and count against your Drive storage. They are not sent anywhere else.
- Haruka keeps the Google access token in memory only and stores no Google password.
- You can stop syncing at any time in Haruka's settings. To delete the sync files, remove Haruka's app data in Google Drive (Settings › Manage apps › Haruka › Delete hidden app data), or revoke access at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

Haruka's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Third-party services

Content comes from extensions you install, which connect to their own websites under those sites' policies. Trackers such as AniList or MyAnimeList receive data only after you log in to them. Haruka checks GitHub (`github.com/hoshinoht/haruka`) for app updates.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at <https://github.com/hoshinoht/haruka/issues>.
